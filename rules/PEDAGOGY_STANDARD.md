# 🎓 Pedagogical Protocol: Standards for Explaining & Teaching Children

Designed for teaching 5th grade mathematics and complex concepts with absolute clarity and engagement:

---

## 1. Physical Mental Model ("The Bag and Candies")
Never use abstract arrows or confusing mechanical tricks without a physical ground:
- **From Large to Small Unit (e.g., kg -> g, km -> m):**
  - **MULTIPLY (×):** When you open a big bag, candies pour out in a LARGE number ➔ zeroes grow, comma shifts right (→).
- **From Small to Large Unit (e.g., mm -> cm, g -> kg):**
  - **DIVIDE (:):** Packing small coins into big boxes yields FEWER boxes ➔ zeroes melt away, comma shifts left (←).
- **The 3-Question Algorithm:**
  1. Which unit is larger?
  2. Which sign (× or :)?
  3. By how much (how many zeroes / comma steps)?

## 2. Realistic 2-3 Step Story Problems
- Ban trivial 1-step examples.
- Craft real-world story problems: baking, sports, hiking, shopping, gardening.
- Require bringing all units to a common measurement first, then calculating the answer.

## 3. Anti-Cheating UI for Interactive Trainers
- **Live Error Counter:** Visible attempt counter (`⚠️ Errors: X`).
- **Locked Solutions:** Solution walkthrough is strictly hidden and locked until the correct answer is entered.
- **Unlock on Correct Answer:** Reveal the verified walkthrough only after success for self-audit.

## 4. Print-Ready (A4) Format
All study guides and cheat sheets must support clean `@media print` with large typography and writing margins.
