# 🏛 Architecture Decision Records (ADR) Standard

Every pivotal architectural, technology, or protocol choice must be documented in lightweight markdown under `docs/adr/NNN-<title>.md`.

---

## Format:
1. **Context:** What engineering challenge or constraint prompted this decision?
2. **Decision:** What specific approach, library, or protocol was chosen?
3. **Alternatives Evaluated:** What other options were considered, and why were they rejected?
4. **Consequences:** Positive and negative trade-offs of the decision.
